Her Story & Mission

A Lifelong Sanctuary for Creative Courage

Movement Science & Pedagogy

Where Artistry Meets Anatomical Intelligence

Phe-be integrates STEM principles, kinesiology, and Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) into every class—proving that the human body is an exquisite laboratory of physics, rhythm, and emotional expression.

The Physics of Elevation & Kinetic Energy

Students do not merely jump—they learn how ground reaction force, center of gravity, and torque create effortless suspension. By understanding musculoskeletal levers, young dancers achieve greater technical height while protecting developing joints.

  • ✓ Angular Momentum in Pirouettes: Understanding mass distribution and rotational inertia for flawless turns.
  • ✓ Deceleration & Joint Safety: Teaching plié mechanics as shock absorbers to ensure lifelong physical durability.
  • ✓ Core Stability & Dynamic Levers: Activating the transverse abdominis for architectural balance and control.
⚡ Kinesiology Principle: Center of Mass & Partner Counterbalance

Rhythm as Living Mathematics & Staging Geometry

Music and choreography are rich applications of fractions, spatial ratios, and syncopated time divisions. In step, tap, and street jazz, students master complex cross-rhythms (3 against 4, 6/8 to 4/4 shifts) while charting geometric stage pathways.

  • ✓ Poly-Meter Subdivision: Layering foot percussions across syncopated backbeats.
  • ✓ Symmetrical & Asymmetrical Formations: Stage geography utilizing tessellations, diagonals, and radial shifts.
  • ✓ Tempo Modulation & Velocity: Connecting acceleration equations to dynamic stage presence.
📐 Rhythmic Math: 8-Count Polyphonic Syncopation

Somatic Regulation & Social-Emotional Intelligence

Dance is neurological co-regulation. Through diaphragmatic breath cycles, grounding circles, and restorative somatic alignment, students release academic stress, calm fight-or-flight responses, and build emotional vocabulary.

  • ✓ Vagus Nerve Stimulation: Incorporating deep breathwork to enhance cognitive focus and emotional composure.
  • ✓ Proprioceptive Sensory Grounding: Tuning fine sensory awareness through barefoot studio exploration.
  • ✓ Trauma-Informed Safe Spaces: Encouraging full authentic vulnerability without judgement or shame.
🌿 Somatic Health: Nervous System Grounding & Vagal Tone

Honoring Ancestral Roots & Cultural Storytelling

Every movement has an origin story. Phe-be educates dancers on the living lineages of West African ring dances, jazz innovators like Katherine Dunham and Alvin Ailey, and urban pioneers who shaped hip-hop and step culture.

  • ✓ African Diaspora Continuum: Tracing polyrhythmic footwork from sacred circles to modern stage works.
  • ✓ Historic Context & Empathy: Understanding how dance has historically served as liberation, healing, and community resilience.
  • ✓ Theatrical Sacred Expression: Conveying reverence and communal storytelling in liturgical repertoires.
🕊️ Cultural Preservation: Sacred Narrative & Theatrical Grace

Inside Phe-be’s Studio

The Classroom as a Transformative Engine

Rather than a rigid checklist, Phe-be orchestrates a 4-phase pedagogical journey in every session—scaffolding emotional safety, technical mastery, individual creative voice, and communal celebration.

01

The Grounding Circle

Every class begins seated together. Students check in emotionally, set a communal intention, and engage in breathwork to leave outside distractions at the studio door.

SEL: Emotional Check-In
02

Technical Architecture

Progressive biomechanical conditioning. From barre alignments to Horton fortifications and polyrhythmic drills, students master precision through positive feedback.

STEM: Musculoskeletal Form
03

Creative Agency & Voice

Students apply technique to original choreography or guided improvisation. They are challenged to create their own 8-counts, collaborate with peers, and express real stories.

Creativity: Youth Choreography
04

Cypher & Affirmation

Class concludes with a supportive cypher circle. Dancers perform for one another, practice constructive peer critique, and close with collective gratitude and applause.

Confidence: Peer Affirmation
