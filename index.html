About Phe-be
"Dance encourages young people to build confidence, discover their creativity, and express themselves with pride."
— Phe-be

Grounded in Movement, Dedicated to Youth

Phe-be Smith is a Bronx-based dance educator and teaching artist with 11+ years of experience teaching children and youth from early childhood through high school. Her experience includes dance education in school and community settings, choreography, performance preparation, and youth development.

Phe-be creates engaging, age-appropriate dance experiences that encourage creativity, confidence, discipline, collaboration, physical awareness, and self-expression while providing students with a strong foundation in movement and dance technique.

Teaching Approach

Technique + Creativity + Student Engagement

Phe-be's classes combine structured technique with creativity and student engagement. Lessons can be adapted to students' developmental levels, school goals, available space, and program length.

01

Warm-Up

Gentle conditioning, alignment exercises, breathwork, and dynamic stretches to prepare the body and focus the mind.

02

Technique & Vocabulary

Foundational movement drills, terminology, alignment, posture, and coordination exercises tailored to the discipline.

03

Across-the-Floor

Traveling movement combinations, turns, leaps, footwork, and spatial navigation across the studio floor.

04

Center Work

Grounded phrasing and combinations emphasizing balance, core strength, musicality, and movement control.

05

Choreography

Learning structured choreography combinations or collaborating on creative student composition and improvisation.

06

Review

Consolidating new movement material, peer encouragement, collaborative sharing, and constructive feedback.

07

Cool Down

Mindful stretching, somatic reset, breathing exercises, and positive group affirmation before dismissing.

📐

Flexible & Context-Specific Structure

The exact class structure is thoughtfully adapted according to: student age, developmental level, dance discipline, program objectives, available space, class duration, and school/program goals.
